# Voke - AI-Powered Interview Preparation Platform
## Comprehensive Business Model

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Vision**: Transform interview preparation through AI-powered personalized coaching, making professional success accessible to everyone.

**Mission**: Empower job seekers with cutting-edge AI technology to practice, improve, and excel in interviews through realistic simulations and actionable feedback.

**Value Proposition**: The only interview preparation platform that combines video analysis, voice AI coaching, adaptive text interviews, peer practice, and personalized learning paths - all powered by advanced AI to deliver professional-grade feedback at a fraction of traditional coaching costs.

## 3. Product Offering

### 3.1 Core Features

#### 🎥 Video Interview Practice
- Record video responses to interview questions
- AI analysis of body language, delivery, confidence
- Detailed scoring and feedback
- Session history and progress tracking
- **Use Case**: Master non-verbal communication

#### 🎙️ Voice AI Interview Coach
- Real-time voice conversation with AI
- Two modes: Interview Practice + General Career Chat
- Context-aware (resume + GitHub profile)
- Natural conversation flow
- **Use Case**: Practice speaking naturally, get instant feedback

#### 💬 Adaptive Text Interviews
- AI-generated personalized questions
- Timed multi-question sessions
- Adaptive difficulty based on performance
- Comprehensive feedback reports
- **Use Case**: Prepare for specific roles/companies

#### 👥 Peer Interview Practice
- Live 1-on-1 sessions with other users
- Role-based matching (interviewer/interviewee)
- Mutual feedback and ratings
- Community building

- Performance tracking over time
- Strengths and weaknesses analysis
- Industry benchmarking

- **Use Case**: Data-driven improvement

#### 🎯 Learning Paths

- **Use Case**: Structured learning journey

#### 🏆 Gamification
- Leaderboards and rankings
- Achievement badges

- **Use Case**: Motivation and engagement

## 4. Master Financial Audit & Revenue Model

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### 4.2 Free Tier Deliverables & Onboarding Gift Cost
Every 1st-time registered user receives 4 Free Credits on onboarding:

- **2 Free Text Interview Credits**: ₹0.34 INR (2 × ₹0.17)
- **2 Free Pro Video Credits**: ₹5.44 INR (2 × ₹2.72)
- **Question Bank Access**: ₹0.00 (Hosted content)
- **Basic Score & 6Q Summary**: Included in interview cost
- **Total Free Gift Cost per User**: **₹5.78 INR / user** ($0.060 USD)
- **Total Free Onboarding Expense (4,000 Users)**: 4,000 × ₹5.78 = **₹23,120 INR** ($240.83 USD)
