# Voke B2B and Partnership Growth Plan

**Plan period:** August 2026 - January 2027  
**Commercial model:** B2B, B2B2C referral, and institution-funded bulk access  
**Primary objective:** acquire placement-ready students at a measurable cost while maintaining positive contribution margin.

---

## 1. Executive decision

Voke should **not** begin by spending heavily on generic sponsorships or by relying on Unstop. A student entering a normal hackathon is focused on building and competing, not interview preparation. The most valuable moment for Voke is when a student is actively preparing for a placement, internship, technical interview, or HR round.

The initial go-to-market priority is:

1. **College placement cells and student placement committees** - highest interview intent; fastest feedback.
2. **Coding clubs, technical societies, bootcamps, and career communities** - good audience fit; lighter approval process.
3. **Hackathons with a job, internship, PPI, or recruiter component** - use them for lead capture and post-event conversion, not broad premium sales.
4. **Large platforms such as Unstop** - approach after Voke has two or three case studies; treat as a campaign/listing partner rather than a guaranteed affiliate channel.

The recommended commercial principle is: **no large upfront partner fee; pay only for verified paid upgrades.**

## 4. Commercial offers

### A. College referral programme - default offer

The college does not need a budget or procurement approval. Voke gives it a unique code and pays a verified referral incentive.

| Plan | Student price | Student saving vs retail | College incentive | Voke receives before direct delivery costs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Voke Pro Campus | INR 329 | INR 70 | INR 32.90 (10%) | INR 296.10 |
| Voke Elite Campus | INR 519 | INR 80 | INR 51.90 (10%) | INR 467.10 |

Offer the incentive as a **placement-cell or student-club success fund**. Never offer personal payments to faculty or public officials. Pay monthly only to an approved organisation account or, where permitted, to the registered student club.

### B. Institution-funded bulk purchase

The college pays once and distributes seats to students. This is true B2B revenue and should be the expansion path after a successful referral pilot.

| Package | Minimum seats | Price per activated student | Delivery cost | Direct contribution before support/overhead |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Voke Pro Placement Pack | 100 | INR 299 | INR 11.39 | INR 287.61 |
| Voke Elite Placement Pack | 100 | INR 449 | INR 31.20 | INR 417.80 |

Terms: 100% prepayment; 90-day activation window; unused seats expire unless the order is renewed; no partner commission on a bulk invoice. Include one launch workshop and an aggregate cohort report. Quote GST separately where applicable.

### C. Community, bootcamp, and hackathon referral offer

| Plan | Partner student price | Partner payout | Why it works |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Voke Pro Partner | INR 349 | INR 52.35 (15%) | INR 50 student discount, easy conversion |
| Voke Elite Partner | INR 549 | INR 82.35 (15%) | Premium practice immediately before interviews |

Use a 15% payout only for a partner that can reliably send students through a tracked code. Cap hackathon payouts at INR 100 per paid sale. Never offer both a 15% platform commission and a 10% university commission on the same transaction; total payout is capped at 20% of collected revenue.

### D. Event lead-capture offer - not a premium-sales offer

For a regular hackathon, give an **Event Practice Pass** instead of the full free bundle:

- 1 Text Interview + 1 Pro Video Interview
- Project Pitch Interview template: explain the problem, technical decisions, impact, trade-offs, teamwork, and learning.
- Internal cost: **INR 2.89 per activated participant**.
- Follow up only with students who complete the practice or explicitly opt in for career preparation.

This is a low-cost way to turn a hackathon audience into qualified placement leads.

| Elite via 10% college referral | INR 519 | INR 10.38 | INR 31.20 | INR 51.90 | **INR 425.52** |

### Recommended payout policy

- Colleges: 10% of collected revenue.
- Communities / bootcamps: 15% of collected revenue.
- Hackathon organisers: 15%, capped at INR 100 per converted student.
- Student ambassadors: 5%, paid from the channel's allocation rather than added on top.
- One student, multiple referrers: total commission capped at 20%.
- Payout eligibility: a successful payment, no refund/chargeback, a valid referral code, and a 14-day hold period.
- Do not use the phrase "profit share" in contracts; use **performance referral commission** with a precise definition of net collected revenue.

## 6. The sales motion

### Step 1: Get the meeting

Contact the placement officer and the student placement lead together. Lead with a no-budget pilot, not with a request to purchase software.

**Subject:** Free interview-readiness sprint for [College] placement students

> Hi [Name], we are Voke, an AI interview-practice platform built for technical placements. We would like to run a 14-day Placement Readiness Sprint for [College] at no upfront cost: a student webinar, practice access, and an aggregate readiness report for your team. Students who want advanced practice can use a campus-only code; the placement cell receives 10% of verified upgrades as a student-success fund. Could we show you the 15-minute pilot plan this week?

### Step 2: Run a 14-day pilot

1. Provide the college its named landing page and referral code.
2. Host a 30-minute webinar: "How to explain projects, DSA reasoning and HR stories in interviews."
3. Give the Event Practice Pass to attendees.
4. Send a personalised follow-up 24 hours later.
5. Run two reminder messages: Day 5 and Day 11.
6. Share only aggregate results with the college; never share an individual student's interview content without their explicit permission.

### Step 3: Convert the pilot to annual/cohort access

After two weeks, present registrations, activations, practice completion, conversion, top improvement areas, and testimonial snippets. Offer a 100-seat paid Placement Pack for the next drive.

## 7. Hiring competitions: how Voke should use them

A hiring competition is a real recruiting funnel, often moving from application to assessment to coding/case study to technical and HR interviews. It is much higher-intent than a general hackathon.

Voke should not ask to access employer candidate data. Instead, use a role-specific public landing page and an opt-in message:

> You cleared the assessment round. Practice explaining your project, approach and HR stories before your next technical interview. Use code [CODE] for INR 50 off Voke Elite for the next 72 hours.

### Campaign: Voke Interview Ready Sprint

| Candidate moment | Message | Offer |
|---|---|---|
| Before applying | Understand the likely interview pattern for the role | Free Text mock |
| Cleared assessment | Prepare for technical and HR rounds now | INR 50 off Pro / Elite |
| Final-round shortlist | Practise project explanation, body language, and resume questions | Elite package + 72-hour expiry |
| Not selected | Keep your feedback and improve for the next opportunity | Re-engagement Pro offer |

### Important platform caution

Unstop has overlapping assessment, interview and AI-interview products. Therefore do not assume that it will distribute Voke as an affiliate. First build independent placement-cell and community case studies; then pitch Unstop a distinct campaign such as a Voke Placement Readiness Challenge or an event listing that produces opt-in leads.

## 8. Product and operations required before selling

The partnership strategy needs a minimum B2B operating layer.

### Must-have before pilot 1

- Unique promo/referral code per partner and per event.
- Landing page that records partner, UTM source, college, campaign, role, and graduation year.
- A conversion dashboard: registrations, activated users, paid users, collected revenue, refunds, commission due.
- Coupon rules: expiry date, plan eligibility, no stacking, one per user.
- Consent checkbox for marketing follow-up and a clear privacy notice.
- Terms stating that Voke is a preparation tool and does not guarantee a job, offer, interview shortlisting, or salary outcome.
- Manual payout spreadsheet and invoice/receipt workflow.

### Build after the first three pilots

- Partner dashboard with clicks, activations, conversions, earnings, and downloadable statement.
- Cohort admin dashboard with seat assignment and aggregate readiness analytics.
- Role/company interview templates: SDE, data analyst, product, consulting, HR.
- Bulk voucher generation and university SSO only after repeatable demand.

## 9. Pilot economics

### 1,000 registration community pilot

Assumptions: 5% paid conversion, 25 Pro and 25 Elite sales, partner prices of INR 349 and INR 549, 15% partner commission, and the lean Event Practice Pass rather than the full free bundle.

| Line item | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| Revenue | (25 x INR 349) + (25 x INR 549) | INR 22,450.00 |
| Event Practice Pass delivery | 1,000 x INR 2.89 | -INR 2,890.00 |
| Paid-plan AI delivery | (25 x INR 11.39) + (25 x INR 31.20) | -INR 1,064.75 |
| Payment gateway fee | 2% x INR 22,450 | -INR 449.00 |
| Partner commission | 15% x INR 22,450 | -INR 3,367.50 |
| **Contribution after direct costs** |  | **INR 14,678.75** |

At a 10% conversion rate, the same 1,000-registration pilot generates **INR 32,247.50** contribution after these direct costs. These figures are not final profit because fixed operating expenses are excluded.

### Pilot approval rules

Continue a channel only if it reaches all of these within 30 days:

- 1,000 registrations or 300 targeted placement-eligible registrations.
- At least 40% of signups activate practice.
- At least 5% of signups convert to paid or at least 12% of activated users convert.
- Refund/chargeback rate below 5%.
- Positive contribution after trial, AI, payment and partner costs.

## 10. Six-month roadmap

### Month 1 - Build and validate

- Create referral codes, landing pages, consent language, payout sheet, and a 12-slide sales deck.
- Recruit 10 target colleges, five student clubs, and three bootcamps.
- Run two no-upfront pilots.

### Months 2-3 - Prove repeatability

- Run 10 college/community pilots.
- Gather three written case studies: registrations, activation, conversion, learner quotes, and aggregate readiness improvement.
- Convert the two best colleges to 100-seat bulk packages.

### Months 4-6 - Scale only successful channels

- Add 20 placement-cell partners and five paid bootcamp/community partners.
- Launch a hiring-competition readiness campaign for role-specific audiences.
- Take case studies to larger platforms and negotiate a limited 90-day campaign, not an open-ended discount.

## 12. Risks and decisions

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Generic hackathon users do not convert | Use hackathons only for low-cost opt-in lead capture and Project Pitch practice. |
| Discounting damages the brand | Keep the discount modest: INR 50-80; make it time-limited and partner-specific. |
| Partner wants a large upfront sponsorship | Offer a 90-day performance pilot first; decline if the economics cannot be tracked. |
| University needs a formal purchase process | Start with referral model, then convert proof into bulk order. |
| Partner disputes sales attribution | Define last-click/unique-code attribution, 14-day hold, and monthly statements in writing. |
| Voke is seen as promising employment | Use clear product language: interview preparation, not a job guarantee. |
| Personal interview data creates trust/privacy concerns | Obtain consent; share only anonymised aggregate cohort analytics with partners. |

## 13. The decision to make now

For the next 30 days, Voke should sell **one simple offer**:

> A no-upfront 14-day Placement Readiness Sprint for colleges and career communities, with an Event Practice Pass, a named partner code, a 10-15% verified-upgrade commission, and an aggregate outcomes report.

Do not begin with a national Unstop sponsorship. Win three placement-cell or community pilots, publish their results, then use those proof points to negotiate with larger platforms.
