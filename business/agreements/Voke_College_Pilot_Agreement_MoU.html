Memorandum of Understanding (MoU)

Institutional Placement Pilot, Feedback Partnership & Brand Authorization

This Memorandum of Understanding ("Agreement") is entered between:

1. VOKE AI (hereinafter "Voke" or "Service Provider"), operating the AI-powered interview readiness & career simulation platform, and

2. College / Partner Institution:

Preamble & Purpose

The Institution strives to enhance student interview readiness and corporate employability. Voke provides a proprietary AI simulation platform conducting real-time video, voice, and technical mock interviews with automated performance diagnostics. The Parties agree to execute a sponsored, zero-cost placement preparation pilot program for designated students in exchange for verified student feedback, internal AI model training rights, and institutional marketing/brand recognition rights.

Clause 1: Scope of Pilot & Free Access (Zero Commercial Consideration)
  • Student Allocation: Voke agrees to grant sponsored access to the Voke Platform for up to _______ Students of the Institution.
  • 100% Free of Cost: This pilot is provided strictly at ₹0 / $0 (Zero Commercial Fee). Neither the Institution nor participating students shall be charged any registration, access, or subscription fees.
  • Platform Inclusions: Enrolled students receive access to AI-driven mock interviews (Text, Voice, and Video practice), real-time evaluation of articulation, technical accuracy, and camera confidence, plus personalized scorecards.
  • Placement Analytics: Voke shall provide the Institution's Placement Cell with an aggregate cohort readiness report highlighting batch participation and overarching skill strengths/gaps.
Clause 2: Student Feedback & Collaboration Obligations (The Consideration)
  • Mandatory Student Feedback: In consideration for receiving sponsored access without financial charge, 100% of participating students who take an interview on Voke shall complete the post-interview diagnostic feedback survey.
  • Scope of Feedback: Feedback shall evaluate platform usability, realism of interview simulations, question relevancy, speech/video evaluation accuracy, and overall learning value.
  • Placement Cell Facilitation: The Institution’s Training & Placement Cell agrees to actively coordinate and mandate feedback submission, targeting a minimum completion rate of 90%+ across the participating student cohort.
  • Institutional Testimonial: Upon completion of the pilot, an authorized representative agrees to provide a brief written review/quote on the platform's utility and placement impact.
Clause 3: AI Model Training, Confidentiality & Strict Non-Sharing Guarantee
  • Internal AI Training Rights: The Institution acknowledges and agrees that Voke shall have the explicit right to process, analyze, and use student feedback, platform ratings, diagnostic metrics, and anonymized interview interaction data internally for the sole purpose of training, fine-tuning, benchmarking, and improving Voke’s proprietary AI models and evaluation algorithms.
  • Strict Non-Disclosure & Non-Sharing Guarantee: Voke explicitly covenants, warrants, and guarantees that:
    • All student feedback and training datasets shall remain strictly confidential and proprietary to Voke.
    • Voke shall NOT share, sell, rent, license, publish, or disclose this data or any individual student responses to any third parties, commercial advertisers, external institutions, or outside entities under any circumstances.
    • All training data will be processed on an anonymized and de-identified basis to guarantee individual student privacy.
Clause 4: Brand, Name & Logo Marketing Authorization
  • Grant of License: The Institution hereby grants Voke a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use the Institution’s official name, acronym, logo, and emblems for marketing, promotional, and brand-building purposes.
  • Authorized Channels: Voke is authorized to display the Institution's logo and name on the official Voke website (e.g., "Partner Campuses" / "Placement Partners"), pitch decks, investor presentations, case studies, whitepapers, social media channels, and corporate brochures.
  • Case Study & Metrics: Voke may highlight aggregate, anonymized pilot results (e.g., "Achieved 82% interview readiness rate") in public case studies.
