1. Problem 1
Top 3 Problems:
  • High Cost & Demo Barriers: 1-on-1 coaching ($150–$300/hr) & mandatory demo calls are unaffordable for freshers.
  • Ignored Non-Verbal Feedback: Text practice misses posture, eye contact, body language, tone, and confidence. Existing AI tools hallucinate or lack structure.
  • The Preparation Gap: Friends are unavailable/inconsistent; YouTube lacks interactive feedback; standard resources fail to personalize to candidate profiles.
Existing Alternatives:
  • Mock interviews with peers/friends (inconsistent quality & availability).
  • YouTube prep videos (passive, zero personalized feedback).
  • LeetCode / HackerRank (code-focused, no soft skills or video).
  • Expensive human coaches asking for mandatory sales demos.
3. Solution 3
Top Features:
  • 🎥 Video & Body Language Analysis: Gemini 2.5 Flash evaluates posture, eye contact, gestures, and confidence in real time.
  • 🎙️ Voice & Text Interview Modes: Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite + Groq for instant multi-modal practice and feedback.
  • 👤 Profile Personalization: Dynamic interviews tailored to Resume, LinkedIn, GitHub, CodeForces & LeetCode profiles.
  • 🚀 Anurag's Journey: Seamless flow from profile upload -> personalized mock interview -> feedback -> AI job recommendations.
6. Key Metrics 6
  • Paid Conversion Rate: Target 10% (200 Pro + 200 Elite / 4,000 user cohort).
  • Unit Gross Margins: 95.1% Pro & 92.8% Elite.
  • Net Profit Margin: 82.15% Net Profit Margin (₹1,63,970 profit on ₹1,99,600 revenue).
  • Break-Even Point: 61 Paid Users (1.5% conversion rate).
